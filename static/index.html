How the risk score is computed

days_of_stock = on_hand ÷ avg_daily_sales · reorder_point = daily_sales × lead_time × 1.25 · risk from coverage ÷ lead time: <0.5× → CRITICAL, <1× → HIGH, <2× → MEDIUM, <4× → LOW, else OK. Suggested order tops stock up to 30 days of cover. Zero-velocity items are flagged honestly, never scored.
